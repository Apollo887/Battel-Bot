# Battel-Bot
Created a functional battel bot with a horizontal spinning blade cable of moveing in any direction. 
Battle Bot Design Brief
Objective:
Build a small, competitive battle bot for robot combat events. The priority is durability, speed, and a weapon that can actually do damage.
Drive System:
Two-wheel rear-drive handles the main movement, with a front wheel added to tighten turning radius and improve responsiveness in the arena.
Chassis:
The frame should be kept compact and tight. Aluminum and plastic are the go-to materials since they keep weight down without sacrificing too much strength. Edges get reinforced with metal screws and duct tape where needed.
Armor:
A mix of metal and reinforced plastic covers the internals. Duct tape fills in the gaps for quick field repairs and light reinforcement between matches.
Weapon:
A front-mounted metal wedge handles the offensive work, either lifting or flipping opponents on contact. The mount can be swapped out for different weapon configurations depending on the matchup.
Electronics:
Basic wiring runs to the drive motors, held in place with electrical tape. The control system is kept simple: drive and weapon activation, nothing overcomplicated.
Aesthetics:
Colored tape and paint make it stand out in the arena. The overall look is angular and aggressive, which honestly fits the function anyway.
Materials:
Aluminum sheets, plastic panels, rubber wheels, metal screws and fasteners, electrical components, duct tape, decorative tape
Tools:
Screwdrivers, wrenches, wire cutters, wire strippers, soldering iron, drill, metal and plastic cutting tools
Performance Goals:

Stays stable and maneuverable on the arena floor
Holds up to direct hits and collisions
Can flip or disable opponents effectively
